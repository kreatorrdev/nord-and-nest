## Final generation prompts

### Ceramic vase hero

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: luxury Scandinavian home decor website hero cutout. Create a photorealistic studio product image of one large sculptural ivory/off-white matte ceramic jug vase, rounded voluptuous body and narrow cylindrical neck with a large loop handle on right. Beautiful tiny natural speckled clay texture. A single graceful olive branch with sparse delicate green leaves rises from the neck and arcs to the upper right. Full object and branch visible with generous padding on every edge, front three-quarter view, object centered. Soft sunlight from upper left. Restrained warm neutral palette. Absolutely transparent background, no backdrop, no pedestal, no text, no watermark, no extra objects. Real premium product photography, natural imperfections, soft grounding shadow.

### Product catalog

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: seamless precisely aligned 4 by 4 catalog contact sheet for a fictional Scandinavian home decor store. Output a square photograph divided into 16 equal square cells, 4 columns and 4 rows, NO borders, NO gutters, NO text or numbers. Each cell has the same plain warm off-white light beige studio backdrop and a SINGLE different centered product photographed with soft natural side light, 20% empty margin around each item; no item crosses its cell. Row 1 left to right: a cream rounded ceramic jug vase with loop handle and one sparse olive branch; a warm beige sculptural mushroom table lamp; a folded rust terracotta linen throw blanket with fringe; a small minimal amber jar candle with cream wax and no label. Row 2: a tall matte terracotta cylindrical vase; a cream ceramic coffee mug with round handle; a folded cream linen cushion; a matte dark charcoal ceramic bowl. Row 3: a pale sculptural ring-shaped ceramic vase; a small pleated ivory table lamp; a terracotta round ceramic dinner plate; a tall amber glass vase. Row 4: a beige textured wool cushion; a cream geometric candle holder; a natural woven seagrass basket; a stacked pair of small beige ceramic espresso cups. Consistent high end editorial product photography, tangible natural textures, no logos, no text, no watermark. Perfect evenly spaced 4x4 matrix, all 16 objects complete and distinct.

Composition panoramic horizontal, wide lens, no people, no text, no watermark.
